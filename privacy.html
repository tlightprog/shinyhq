Privacy Policy

This policy explains how ShinyHQ, LLC handles information for our company website. Each of our apps has its own product privacy policy that governs the data that app collects.

3. What this website collects

Our website is intentionally minimal. You don’t need an account to browse shinyhq.com, and we don’t sell your data. The site may process:

  • Standard server logs — your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request, kept by our hosting provider for security and reliability.
  • Messages you send us — if you email support@shinyhq.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include, and use it only to respond.
  • Fonts — the site loads typefaces from Google Fonts; your browser’s request to Google is subject to Google’s privacy policy.

We do not run advertising trackers, set advertising cookies, or build advertising profiles on shinyhq.com.

4. How we use information

  • To respond to your questions and support requests.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the website.
  • To comply with applicable law and protect our rights.

5. Sharing & selling

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us run the website (for example, our hosting and font providers) and where required by law or to protect against fraud or abuse.

6. Data retention

We keep support emails for as long as needed to handle your request and maintain our business records. Server logs are retained for a limited period by our hosting provider and then rotated out.

7. Your choices & rights

You can contact us at support@shinyhq.com to ask what information we hold about you, request a correction, or request deletion. For information you’ve entered inside one of our apps, please use that app’s in-app controls and the contact in its own privacy policy.

8. Children

Our website and apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.
